# Cold Outreach Messages — Networking Track

**Goal:** Reach out to 10 people who already recognize your value. Ask them: "What problem would you hire me to solve?"

**Why this works:** Your Ikigai report says your ideal path is **invitation-based, diagnosis-led consulting** — not cold-pursuing. These messages are warm, specific, and ask for insight (not a job).

---

## Template 1: Former Colleague / Client (Warm)

**Subject:** Quick question — what problem would you hire me to solve?

Hi [Name],

Hope you're doing well. It's been a while since we worked together at [shared project/company].

I'm exploring a new direction — moving from hands-on event production into operations consulting, where I help organizations diagnose bottlenecks and fix workflows. Before I commit to a specific path, I wanted to ask the people who know my work best:

**If you could hire me to solve one problem, what would it be?**

No pressure to respond — but if you have a thought, I'd genuinely value it. And if you hear of anyone looking for an operations consultant or fractional PM, I'd appreciate an intro.

Thanks for the time — and for being part of my professional journey.

Best,
Abdullah

## Template 2: Industry Contact (Slightly Less Warm)

**Subject:** Operations consulting — would you hire me for this?

Hi [Name],

I hope you're well. We crossed paths at [event/company/context] and I've always admired your work in [their field].

I'm transitioning from live event production into operations consulting — helping organizations diagnose bottlenecks, redesign workflows, and improve efficiency. My background: 6+ years managing production and site operations for F1, FIFA, Riyadh Fashion Week, and Neom Beach Games. PMP-trained, NEBOSH-certified.

Before I fully commit to this direction, I wanted to ask: **would you ever hire someone with my background to solve an operations problem at your company?** If not, what's missing?

Genuinely open to feedback — no pitch here, just trying to calibrate.

Best,
Abdullah
[LinkedIn URL]

## Template 3: Mentor / Advisor (Deeper Relationship)

**Subject:** Career direction — seeking your perspective

Hi [Name],

I'm at a career inflection point. After 6+ years in event production and operations (F1, FIFA, Riyadh Fashion Week, Neom Beach Games), I'm pivoting toward **human-centric operations consulting** — diagnosing organizational inefficiencies, fixing workflows, and delivering sprint-based projects.

My Ikigai analysis points to this path: invitation-based, diagnosis-led, small client rooms, autonomy. It aligns with what I've been best at — root-cause analysis, talent-to-role placement, and redesigning processes under pressure.

Before I go all-in, I'd value your perspective:
1. **Does this direction make sense given what you've seen of my work?**
2. **Would you ever hire me (or recommend me) for this type of role?**
3. **Who else should I talk to?**

I'm not asking for a job — I'm asking for honest feedback from someone whose opinion I respect.

Thank you,
Abdullah
[LinkedIn URL] · [Phone]
